Working in decibels

Two equal sources add to +3 dB, ten equal sources to +10 dB. When levels differ by more than 10 dB, the quieter source is effectively lost in the sum. Removing the loudest machine in a plant therefore rarely yields the improvement intuition suggests — check the numbers here first.

Typical operating points

Diagnostic imaging: 1–20 MHz (higher frequency → better resolution, more attenuation, shallower depth). NDT: 0.5–15 MHz with couplant. Soft tissue c ≈ 1540 m/s is the standard assumed value; the near field (Fresnel zone) collimates the beam, while beyond it the beam diverges at roughly sinθ ≈ 1.22 λ/D.

Dispersion in guides

Below cutoff the mode is evanescent and decays exponentially. Above cutoff, phase velocity always exceeds c while group velocity is always below c, with vp·vg = c². Single-mode operation requires f between the dominant and next-higher cutoff — for a rectangular guide that is a band of roughly one octave (TE₁₀ to TE₂₀/TE₀₁).
