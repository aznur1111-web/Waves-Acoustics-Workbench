Working in decibels

Two equal sources add to +3 dB, ten to +10 dB. When levels differ by more than 10 dB the quieter source is effectively lost in the sum — removing the loudest machine in a plant rarely yields the improvement intuition suggests. Check the numbers here first, then confirm against the governing occupational standard.
